One-line reminders of what each MISRA C:2012 rule is about, for the messages of
`scripts/cppcheck.py --misra`.  The numbers and categories are MISRA's; the wording is this
project's own shorthand, not the text of the guidelines, and it leaves out their conditions
and exceptions.  The guidelines themselves are published by MISRA (misra.org.uk); pass a
copy of their summary appendix with --rule-texts to get the official headlines instead.

The lines below are in the layout cppcheck's addon reads: every reminder is one line and
starts with a capital letter or '#'.
